## Current conference instructions

- Printed posters use A0 portrait, 841 by 1189 mm.
- PDF uploads have a 20 MB limit.
- Authors may use their own poster design. The FAQ supplies no mandatory template.
- Upload filenames should identify the conference and paper. The deliverable
  keeps AACL_2026_MultiPsyche_2_poster.pdf for this purpose.
- The material submission deadline is 28 October 2026.

The visible header identifies the workshop and conference. The paper number
remains in the upload filename.

The general conference slide deck lists several presentation materials.
MultiPsyche's later workshop email makes posters and videos optional and
encourages at least a poster. That workshop-specific message governs the choice
of materials for this paper.

## Comparison with recent ACL events

The [ACL 2025 FAQ](https://2025.aclweb.org/faq/) also specifies A0 portrait for
main-conference and workshop posters.
The [ACL 2024 printing guidance](https://2024.aclweb.org/participants/printing/)
recommends margins on all sides and 300 ppi raster images. These are useful print checks, rather than additional
AACL-IJCNLP 2026 submission rules.

This poster uses 30 pt body text, vector plots, embedded fonts and an A0 page.
The small Santa Casa raster mark is placed at approximately 400 ppi.
The remaining active logos and both QR codes use vector artwork.
The two QR destinations and the clickable links are checked in the final PDF.
