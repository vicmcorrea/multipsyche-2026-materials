\sectiontitle{28}{218}{374}{Introduction}
\place{28}{253}{374}{\fs{30}{38}
  Workplace surveys often contain brief or repeated answers.
  Translation makes these responses accessible to English-centered
  analysis tools, including resources used in mental-health research.
  \par\vspace{7mm}
  We examine what reference-free audit tools reveal about
  Portuguese-to-English survey translations and how response length,
  repetition and lexical coverage shape their interpretation.}

\sectiontitle{28}{357}{374}{Data and methods}
\place{28}{394}{374}{\fs{30}{38}
  The corpus contains \textbf{44,118 response pairs} from
  \textbf{14,706 survey-specific records} across four Brazilian
  workplace survey cohorts from 2023 to 2025.
  Each record contributes three answers.
  One third of pairs come from mental-health or well-being prompts.}
\place{28}{464}{374}{\fs{30}{38}
  \textbf{Paired-text retrieval.}
  Qwen3-Embedding-4B [1] ranks all 44,118 candidates in the other
  language. The paired target or an exact normalized duplicate
  counts as a correct match. We also tested the 8B encoder.}
\place{28}{524}{374}{\fs{30}{38}
  \textbf{Quality estimation.}
  COMETKiwi DA-XL [2] estimates translation quality from
  the Portuguese source and English translation.}
\place{28}{568}{374}{\fs{30}{38}
  \textbf{Language and affective coverage.}
  Lingua provides language flags. EmoAtlas [3] measures
  emotion-lexicon coverage in each language.}
\place{28}{612}{374}{\color{Muted}\fs{27}{34}
  Retrieval, quality estimation and coverage are interpreted separately.}

\sectiontitle{28}{688}{374}{Interpretation}
\place{28}{725}{374}{\fs{30}{38}
  Retrieval measures how distinguishable a paired text is within
  this corpus. A missed match can reflect brief or repeated wording.
  \par\vspace{7mm}
  COMETKiwi follows a different pattern across response lengths.
  Reporting the measures separately keeps their meanings clear.
  Bilingual human ratings would calibrate the quality estimates.}

\sectiontitle{28}{833}{374}{Limitations}
\place{28}{870}{374}{\fs{30}{38}
  Results describe one language pair and one workplace-survey
  setting. Translation provenance is incomplete, and encoder
  comparisons remain within the Qwen3 family.
  \par\vspace{7mm}
  Assessing translation accuracy and emotional or cultural
  equivalence requires human assessment and validation in
  the target domain.}
\place{28}{1001}{374}{\color{Muted}\fs{25}{32}
  Survey comments remain confidential.
  The public code includes frozen configurations and synthetic tests.}
