# Institutional marks

The NILC and ICMC-USP vector marks come from the author's
[qualification slides](https://github.com/vicmcorrea/slidesThesisSemanticDriftQualification).
They retain their original artwork.

- nilc.pdf comes from figs/logos/icmc_nilc_logo4-eps-converted-to.pdf.
- icmc-usp.pdf comes from figs/logos/LOGO_ICMC_CMYK-eps-converted-to.pdf.

The poster also follows the slides' white background, NILC blue
3331B4 and thin heading rules. Its serif typography and two-column layout
follow the academic-poster examples supplied with the revision request.
